Text Message Consent

How we collect permission to text our wedding guests, what we send, and how to stop the messages at any time.

Who is sending these messages

Austin Sabella and Anastasiia Oliinyk, a couple getting married on April 23, 2027 in Chicago, Illinois. We send wedding messages from +1 (833) 300‑0423 to our invited guests only: family members and close friends. This is a personal wedding, not a business. Nothing we send is promotional, and we never sell, share, or rent anyone's phone number.

How consent is collected

Every phone number on our list was given to us personally by that guest, in conversation, for the purpose of hearing about our wedding. When we collect a number, this is the language we use:

"Can we get your number so we can text you our save-the-date and updates about the wedding, like RSVP reminders and details for the day?"

We only add a number after the guest says yes.

Example of how consent happens

Austin: We're putting the guest list together for the wedding. Can we use this number to text you the save-the-date and occasional updates, like RSVP reminders and day-of details?

Guest: Of course, this number works.

Austin: Great. You can reply STOP to any message if you ever want us to quit texting you.

What we send

  • One save-the-date with a link to our wedding website
  • Occasional RSVP reminders for guests who have not yet responded
  • Logistics for the day itself: venue, timing, and weather plans

We expect to send about six messages in total between now and April 2027. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

How consent is recorded and stored

Each guest's name, phone number, when the number was given to us, and their opt-out status are stored in a private, access-controlled database that only the two of us can read. Numbers are used solely for the wedding messages described above and are deleted after the wedding.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message and you will be removed immediately and permanently. You can also reply HELP for assistance, or write to us at the email below and we will remove you by hand. Opting out never affects your invitation. We will just reach you another way.
