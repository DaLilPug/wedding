Questions

When should I RSVP?

Ideally, the moment you finish reading this sentence. Search your name below and your whole crew appears together. And if your plans change later, tell us ASAP - we would rather redo the seating chart than pay for a ghost's dinner.

Can I bring a guest?

Search your name below - everyone your invitation covers shows up together. If someone you love is missing from the list, text us and plead your case.

Where do I park?

The 55th Street lot is closest. From there it's a short walk through the underpass, or flag down our golf cart, which will be running all evening. Yes, there will be a golf cart. We're excited about it too.

Indoors or outdoors?

Ceremony outdoors on the lakefront, dinner and dancing inside the historic field house. April by the lake has opinions - bring a layer.

What should I wear?

We're currently planning on formal attire, with full details to follow closer to the wedding. Two colors are off the table: white and red. Wear either and the bridesmaids will attack. This is not a joke. We cannot guarantee your safety.

Are kids invited?

We love your kids, but we're keeping the evening adults only. Think of it as a night off: dress up, stay late, and let the babysitter handle bedtime.

Any wedding rules I should know about?

The bride is Ukrainian, so a strict set of traditions will be enforced. These include, but are not limited to: loud partying, potential fights, and at least one embarrassing moment per guest. Participation is mandatory.
