Where

Promontory Point

A peninsula on Lake Michigan

A 1930s lakefront park jutting into Lake Michigan, with the Chicago skyline floating across the water. The ceremony and reception are both here — one place, all evening.

5491 S. DuSable Lake Shore Drive
Chicago, IL 60615
Getting there15 minutes from the Loop. Rideshare drops at 55th Street; the underpass walk takes 5 minutes.
ParkingLot at 55th Street (pay-to-park). A golf cart will shuttle anyone who'd rather ride than walk.

Questions

When should I RSVP?

As soon as you can — it truly helps us plan. Search your name below; your whole party appears together.

Can I bring a guest?

Your invitation covers everyone listed in your party when you search your name. If you have a question about a plus-one, just text us.

Where do I park?

The lot at 55th Street is closest (pay-to-park). From there it's a short walk through the underpass — or catch the golf cart we'll have running all evening.

Indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony is outdoors on the lakefront; dinner and dancing are inside the historic field house. April evenings by the lake run cool — bring a layer.

What should I wear?

Dress code details will follow with the formal invitation. Think festive and comfortable enough to dance.

Are kids invited?

Details to follow with the formal invitation — reach out if it changes your plans.
