Austin & Ana Sabella Wedding Updates. A personal, non-commercial text message program run by Austin Sabella and Anastasiia Oliinyk for the guests invited to their wedding on April 23, 2027 at Promontory Point in Chicago, Illinois. Invited wedding guests only, roughly 115 family members and friends, each of whom personally gave us their mobile number and verbally agreed to receive wedding updates. The full consent script and an example of how consent is obtained are on our SMS consent page. We do not add anyone who did not give us their number directly.

Message frequency

Recurring messages, roughly six in total between now and April 2027. Message frequency varies.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We charge nothing. Any charges come from your own mobile carrier under your plan.
