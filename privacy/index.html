Privacy Policy

Last updated August 25, 2026

Who we are

Austin Sabella and Anastasiia Oliinyk, a couple getting married on April 23, 2027 in Chicago, Illinois. This website exists for one purpose: sharing wedding details with our invited guests. It is not a business and we sell nothing.

What we collect

  • Your name, and the names of others in your party
  • Your mobile phone number, given to us personally by you
  • Your email address and mailing address, if you choose to give them when you RSVP
  • Your RSVP response and any note you write us

How we use it

Only to plan and communicate about our wedding: sending you the save-the-date, RSVP reminders, and details for the day; giving the caterer a head count; and mailing you an invitation. Nothing else.

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your information with anyone. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers collected for SMS are never shared with any third party.

Text messages

If you gave us your mobile number, you may receive occasional wedding text messages from us. Details of how that consent was collected are on our SMS consent page, and the program terms are in our Terms. You can stop the messages at any time by replying STOP.

How your information is stored

Guest information lives in a private, access-controlled database that only the two of us can read. The website itself sets no advertising or tracking cookies, and we run no analytics or advertising software on it.

How long we keep it

Until shortly after the wedding, at which point guest contact information is deleted.

Your choices

Write to us at the email below and we will show you what we have, correct it, or delete it. Reply STOP to any text to be removed from text messages permanently.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 except a child's name supplied by their own parent as part of a family's RSVP.
